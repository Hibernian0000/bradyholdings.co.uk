Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Who We Are

Brady Holdings (“we” or “us”) is a privately owned investment holding company. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information in connection with bradyholdings.co.uk (the “Site”), which provides background information about Brady Holdings and a contact form.

For the purposes of UK data protection law, including the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, Brady Holdings is the controller of personal information collected through the Site. You can reach us at any time through our contact page.

Information We Collect

“Personal information” means any information that relates to an identifiable individual, such as a name or email address. We collect only what is needed to operate the Site and respond to you.

  1. Enquiries: If you use the contact form, we receive the details you provide: your name, email address, organisation (if given), subject and message. You choose what to include, and we ask that you do not send sensitive personal or financial information through the form.
  2. Technical information: When you visit the Site, our hosting and security providers automatically process technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type and the pages requested. This is needed to deliver the Site and keep it secure.

You do not need to create an account or provide any information to browse the Site.

Cookies and Tracking

The Site does not use analytics, advertising or profiling technologies, and does not set cookies of its own. Our infrastructure providers may use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies solely to deliver the Site securely and reliably. These are not used to track you across other websites.

How We Use Your Information

We use personal information only to:

  1. respond to enquiries you send us;
  2. keep a record of our correspondence with you;
  3. maintain the security and integrity of the Site; and
  4. comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

Our lawful basis for the first three purposes is our legitimate interest in operating the Site and responding to enquiries made to us. For the last, it is compliance with a legal obligation.

We do not sell personal information, use it for marketing or solicitation, or make decisions about you by automated means.

Who We Share Information With

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run the Site and handle enquiries, such as providers of website hosting, content delivery and security, and form processing and email delivery. We choose providers that apply appropriate security and data protection standards, and they may use personal information only to provide their services to us.

We may also disclose personal information where the law requires it, or where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal rights. We do not otherwise share it.

International Transfers

Some of our service providers operate internationally, so personal information may be processed outside the United Kingdom, including in the United States. Where this happens, the transfer is protected by safeguards recognised under UK data protection law, such as adequacy regulations or standard contractual clauses.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep personal information only for as long as it is needed for the purposes described above, including dealing with your enquiry, keeping a record of it and meeting any legal obligations. Technical information is retained by our providers in line with their normal operations. When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

The Site is intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please tell us through our contact page and we will delete it promptly.

Your Rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask whether we hold personal information about you, and request a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information;
  • ask us to restrict how we use your information; and
  • object to us using your information on the basis of our legitimate interests.

These rights are not absolute and some apply only in certain circumstances. To exercise any of them, or to ask a question about this policy, please use our contact page. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request, and we will respond within the time required by law, normally one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk. We would appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy if our practices or the law change. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.

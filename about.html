Private Investment Office

Brady Holdings is a privately owned investment holding company established to oversee the long-term financial interests of a family with roots in Ireland and ties to the United Kingdom.

The firm takes a selective, long-term approach to capital stewardship, with an emphasis on capital preservation, measured growth and intergenerational continuity.

Its activities focus principally on the management and investment of privately held family capital across public markets and selected investment opportunities.

Brady Holdings operates exclusively for its own account. It does not seek external investment capital, provide investment advice or investment management services to third parties.
